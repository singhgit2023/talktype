# TalkType 0.3.0

The first public preview of TalkType, a small macOS menu bar companion for voice typing.

- Hold the chosen shortcut to dictate into the focused text field. Words appear as recognition arrives.
- The floating companion follows the latest word smoothly on one line and can be dragged anywhere.
- Choose from seven soft themes, adjust the transcript size, and control mascot motion and sound cues.
- The welcome guide covers permissions and shortcut setup. Settings includes Sparkle update controls.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. This free preview uses a persistent local signing certificate and is not Apple notarized. macOS may ask you to allow it in Privacy & Security after downloading.
