# TalkType 0.3.2

- Simplify the menu bar popover to the mascot and app name, microphone status, Settings, and Quit.
- Keep voice typing available through the configured hold shortcut, with update checks and floating companion controls in Settings.
- Preview speech in the floating companion while the shortcut is held, then insert the complete formatted transcript into the focused text field after the shortcut is released.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. This free preview uses a persistent local signing certificate and is not Apple notarized. macOS may ask you to allow it in Privacy & Security after downloading.
